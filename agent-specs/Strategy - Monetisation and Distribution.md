## Layer 1: Awareness (free, public)

### GitHub repo: `product-leader-agents`
**What to publish:**
- All 25 AI skills (the SKILL.md prompts — the actual usable part)
- The Agent Ecosystem diagram
- The PM Stack mapping
- The 4 articles

**Why free on GitHub:**
- AI skills are tools, not content. Making them free builds adoption and trust.
- Every skill includes "Find the full blueprint at productbuilders.pro" — driving traffic to the paid product.
- GitHub stars = social proof. A repo with 500+ stars becomes a credentialing asset.
- AI skills shared on GitHub get forked, modified, and shared again — organic distribution.

**What NOT to publish on GitHub:**
- The PM Blueprints themselves (these are the premium content)
- The company playbooks
- The reference databases
- Templates and frameworks extracted from blueprints
- The AI Skills library documentation (only individual skill prompts)

## Layer 2: Engagement (free, email-gated)

### The lead magnet: "The PM AI Skills Starter Kit"

A free download (email required) containing:
- 5 AI skills (Meeting Prep, Action Extractor, Status Update Writer, Decision Doc Writer, The Council)
- The Agent Ecosystem diagram
- A quickstart guide: "Set up your first AI agent in 30 minutes"

**Why these 5:** They're the daily drivers — the ones any PM can use immediately. Once someone uses Meeting Prep and gets value, they want the other 20.

### The newsletter

A weekly email that delivers:
- One blueprint highlight (a framework or insight from the library)
- One agent tip (how to get more from a specific agent)
- One "building in public" update (what's new in the system)
- One tool recommendation (from the PM Stack, with context)

The newsletter is the nurture engine. Free subscribers see enough value to consider the subscription. Every edition ends with "Get the full blueprint at productbuilders.pro."

## The moat

Why this is hard to copy:

1. **Depth.** 53 blueprints sourced from 640+ Lenny episodes, 12 company playbooks, 6 reference databases. This took months to build. A competitor starting today is months behind.

2. **The skills layer.** Nobody else has mapped PM competencies to AI agent specifications. The blueprints tell you WHAT to do. The skills help the agents DO it for you. That combination is unique.

3. **Living content.** This isn't a course that goes stale. Blueprints update monthly. AI skills evolve with new capabilities. The PM Stack tracks tool changes. Subscribers get a library that improves, not depreciates.

4. **Authentic builder story.** NikoVJ's personal brand — building in public, showing the real system, sharing failures — creates trust that corporate brands can't manufacture.

5. **The Storymapper.** When built, it's a software tool that makes the subscription sticky. You don't churn from a tool you use weekly.
