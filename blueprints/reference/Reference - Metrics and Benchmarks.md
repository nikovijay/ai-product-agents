## Free-to-Paid Conversion Benchmarks

| Model | Good | Great | Context | Source |
|-------|------|-------|---------|--------|
| Freemium self-serve | 3%-5% | 6%-8% | Canva, Trello, Typeform | `what-is-good-free-to-paid-conversion.md` |
| Freemium with sales-assist | 5%-7% | 10%-15% | Airtable, GitLab, HubSpot | `what-is-good-free-to-paid-conversion.md` |
| Free trial | 8%-12% | 15%-25% | Shopify, Google Workspace, Intercom | `what-is-good-free-to-paid-conversion.md` |
| Reverse trial | ~2x freemium | -- | Only 5% of products use this; converts at 2x classic freemium | `what-is-good-free-to-paid-conversion.md` |

**Key data points:**
- 20% of freemium products see conversion below 2.5%
- 33% of freemium products see between 2.5% and 5% (most common bucket)
- Only ~15% of freemium products exceed 20% conversion
- Developer-focused products: median conversion is 5% -- half that of non-dev products
- Sign-up rates: free-trial products 5%, freemium products 9%
- 44% of free-trial companies have sales reach out to >50% of sign-ups (vs. 24% of freemium)
- 40%-60% of new users never return after the first day

Source: `what-is-good-free-to-paid-conversion.md` (survey of 1,000+ products with Kyle Poyar/OpenView)

### Payback by Business Model

| Business Model | Target Payback | Source |
|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Low loyalty + low frequency (e.g. airfares) | 1-7 days | `what-is-a-good-payback-period.md` |
| Low loyalty + high frequency (e.g. games) | 1-3 months | `what-is-a-good-payback-period.md` |

**Formula:** Payback period = CAC / Monthly Gross Profit (not revenue -- subtract COGS)

## Marketplace Benchmarks

### Core Marketplace Metrics (Priority Order)

1. **Fill rate** -- % of intentful sessions that convert (the ultimate marketplace health metric)
2. **Bookings growth** -- Completed transactions per week/month
3. **Supply growth** -- New active supply per week/month
4. **GMV growth** -- Dollars through the system per week/month

Source: `the-most-important-marketplace-metrics-to-track.md`

> **Take rate = Convenience + Demand - Competition**

## Key Formulas & Definitions

| Formula | Definition | Source |
|---------|------------|--------|
| Activation rate | [users who hit activation milestone] / [users who completed signup] | `what-is-a-good-activation-rate.md` |
| Payback period | CAC / Monthly Gross Profit | `what-is-a-good-payback-period.md` |
| Free-to-paid conversion | [accounts paying within 6 months] / [total new accounts] | `what-is-good-free-to-paid-conversion.md` |
| Net revenue retention | Current MRR from cohort / MRR 12 months ago from same cohort | `what-is-good-retention.md` |
| SaaS Quick Ratio | (New MRR + Expansion MRR) / (Contraction MRR + Churned MRR) | `the-most-important-bottom-up-saas-metrics-to-track.md` |
| Burn Multiple | Net Burn / Net New ARR | `how-to-know-if-youve-got-product-market-fit.md` |
